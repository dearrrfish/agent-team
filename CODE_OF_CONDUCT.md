# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing experience levels.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project and its users.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are
not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [im@yjin.dev](mailto:im@yjin.dev). Include
links or screenshots and enough context to assess the incident. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant according to the
severity and pattern of behavior.

Anyone responsible for enforcing this code must disclose conflicts of interest
and avoid participating in decisions where impartiality is compromised.
